Arrow Clear is a relaxing one-tap puzzle about untangling a board full of long, colorful snakes of arrows.

Every arrow flies straight in its own direction. Tap one and it launches off the board — unless another arrow is in the way, and then it bumps. Clear every arrow to win the level. Fewer bumps, more stars.

HOW TO PLAY
· Study each arrow's direction and what blocks its path
· Free the blocking arrows first, then peel the board layer by layer
· Clear the whole board to finish the level
· The fewer wall bumps, the more stars you earn

FEATURES
· 500 handcrafted levels across 5 worlds and 25 chapters
· Puzzle-piece boards: clearing a level reveals a hidden picture
· Giant boss arrows that need a two-lane-wide corridor to escape
· Sprint stages, boss rushes and a calm no-fail finale for every chapter
· Bonus challenge: one extra-tricky board, open anytime
· Smart hints that light up an arrow free to fly right now

RELAXED PROGRESSION
· 3 hearts per stage — mistakes cost a heart, never your progress
· Energy refills over time, even offline
· Extra hints, energy and a second chance are there when you need them, free of charge
· Every level is guaranteed solvable — no dead ends, ever

No in-app purchases. No coins. Just arrows, logic and a satisfying whoosh as the board empties.
